# Mode: deep — Deep Research Prompt

Generate a structured prompt for Perplexity/Claude/ChatGPT with 6 axes:

```text
## Deep Research: [Company] — [Role]

Context: I am evaluating a candidacy for [role] at [company]. I need actionable information for the interview.

### 1. Mission and how they deliver it
- What does the organization do, and for whom (customers, clients, investors, users, the public)?
- What services, programs, products or locations does this role touch?
- Where do they talk about their work? (blog, newsletter, annual report, news, social media, conference talks)

### 2. Recent moves (last 6 months)
- Relevant hires, openings or leadership changes in this team or department?
- New programs, sites, products, contracts or partnerships?
- Funding, budget, enrollment or ownership changes?
- Any reorganization, merger or expansion?

### 3. Team culture and ways of working
- How does this team do its day-to-day work? (planning cycles, release or close calendars, tools, processes)
- Which tools, systems, methods or certifications does the team rely on?
- Remote, hybrid or on-site? Schedule and on-call expectations?
- What do employee reviews (Glassdoor, Indeed, field-specific forums) say about this team or role?

### 4. Likely challenges
- What pressures does the organization face? (growth, staffing, budget, regulation, competition, outcomes)
- What problems is this role probably being hired to solve?
- Are they changing anything big? (systems, processes, locations, services)
- What pain points do people mention in reviews?

### 5. Competitors and differentiation
- Who are their main competitors?
- What sets them apart (reputation, quality, mission, people, reach)?
- How are they positioned vs competitors?

### 6. Candidate angle
Given my profile (read from cv.md and profile.yml for specific experience):
- What unique value do I bring to this team?
- Which of my experiences, projects or placements are most relevant?
- What story should I tell in the interview?
```

Personalize each section with the specific context of the job being evaluated.
