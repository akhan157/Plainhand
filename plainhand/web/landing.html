Get started

Install on your computer

In Terminal:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/akhan157/Plainhand/main/install.sh | sh

In PowerShell:

irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/akhan157/Plainhand/main/install.ps1 | iex

Needs Node.js 22 or later. Run the command again later to upgrade; your workspace is kept. Everything stays in ~/.plainhand/workspace. Read the source on GitHub.

FAQ

Questions people ask first.

Does Plainhand submit applications for me?

No. It fills the form, shows a pre-submit list, and stops. You press Submit, then mark the application as sent.

Do I need an API key?

Not on the website. You connect the Claude or ChatGPT plan you already have, and that plan's usage limits apply. On your own computer, it works with Claude, ChatGPT or Google Antigravity (Gemini).

Where does my data live?

On the website, in your hosted Plainhand workspace, encrypted at rest. You can export it or delete your account. On your computer, in ~/.plainhand/workspace. Neither stores job-site passwords.

Can it apply on LinkedIn or Indeed?

No. Their terms prohibit automation, so Plainhand never opens them in its browser. Save the posting there and give Plainhand the employer's application link.

Will my resume look like a template?

No. Plainhand reads your own resume PDF and uses its fonts, margins, headings, section order, dates and bullets for every resume and cover letter it writes.

Will it sound like AI wrote it?

Every line goes through a humanizer pass that removes AI writing patterns without changing a fact or number. Calibration then checks each line against your evidence.

Is it open source?

Yes, under the MIT license. The code is on GitHub, and you can run it on your own computer with one command.
