# Product definition

Decided 2026-11-14. This page is the reference for prompts, interface text, demo
data, benchmark personas and feature scope. When a change conflicts with it,
change the page first, on purpose.

## Who Plainhand is for

Professionals who apply through online application systems with a resume:

- **Finance and accounting:** analysts, accountants, FP&A, risk, banking.
- **Technology:** software, data, IT, product.
- **STEM:** every engineering discipline, research and laboratory science.
- **Similar office and knowledge work:** business operations, consulting,
  analytics, marketing, design, content.

At any career stage, from intern to director. A degree is common but not
assumed; licenses and certifications (CPA, CFA, PE, FE, security or cloud
certifications) count as first-class evidence.

**Out of scope:** nursing and clinical roles, skilled trades, retail and other
hourly or shift work. They use different job sites and different application
conventions. Do not use them as examples, personas or demo content. Nothing in
the app should break for such a user, but nothing is designed or measured for
them.

## Principles

1. **The candidate's field comes from the candidate.** No prompt, default or
   score assumes a field, a degree or a career stage. Engineering is one field
   among several.
2. **A master record, not a standard resume.** The candidate approves one
   complete record of what they have done, in wording they stand behind. Every
   application selects, orders and adapts from that record for the job. Nothing
   relevant is left out because an earlier, general resume left it out.
3. **Works where it works; manual everywhere else.** On application systems the
   app supports, it fills the form and stops before submitting. On any other
   site, including job boards with their own apply flow, it prepares the
   tailored resume, cover letter and answers, and the candidate applies by
   hand and confirms. A job from an unsupported site is never refused.
4. **Unchanged:** every claim is backed by the candidate's own evidence, the
   candidate reviews everything, sensitive questions are theirs to answer, and
   the candidate submits.

## What "manual" means

For a job whose site the app cannot drive:

- The job can still be saved, evaluated and tailored like any other.
- The Apply step shows a manual kit: the posting link, the tailored resume and
  cover letter to download, and each prepared answer with a copy button.
- The candidate applies on the site themselves and marks the application as
  submitted; tracking and follow-ups continue as usual.
- The browser extension, where installed, can fill the open page from the same
  approved answers. That is still the candidate's own browser and their own
  click to submit.

## Measuring it

The benchmark personas, jobs and forms cover this audience and no other:
finance, technology, STEM and adjacent professional roles, from new graduate to
senior manager, with and without licenses. A change that helps one field and
hurts another shows up as a failing scenario.
